HOW TO PLAY

Four rules. Plenty of betrayal.

01
🎴

Wear your Curse

A random Curse Card is placed above your head. Everyone can see it except you.

02
🎵

Dance & deceive

When music plays, keep moving. Bluff, bait, imitate and mislead the other Zombies.

03
⏸️

Freeze

When the music stops, freeze immediately. Clear movement after the stop can eliminate you.

04
💀

Avoid your own Curse

Perform your own forbidden move and you are out. Each round lasts up to 10 minutes; if multiple Zombies remain, a Final Challenge decides the winner.

THE MIND GAME

Make them do it.

You are allowed to talk, bluff and tell another player to perform a move. They choose whether to trust you.

REMEMBER

Your Curse is always active.

Even while calling out another player, your own Curse still applies. Point at someone while your Curse is POINT? You may eliminate yourself.
